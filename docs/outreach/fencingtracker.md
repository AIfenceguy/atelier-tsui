**Subject:** Using your published bout histories in a family training app — asking first

Hello,

I am a fencing parent (two boys, foil, Y12 and Y14 / Cadet) and a long-time reader of
FencingTracker. Over the past year I built a small web app for our family called En Garde: a
training journal and season planner. When one of my sons loses a bout at a competition, the app
pulls the facts of that bout (opponent, score, round) from his published record so he only has
to write what he learned.

Today the app reads FencingTracker pages only when we save them by hand from the browser and
import them. Nothing crawls your site. A few families at our club have asked to use the app, and
before I offer it to them I want to ask how you would like this handled:

1. May a family's own app read a fencer's public bout history and strength on request (one page
   at a time, identified user agent, cached so a page is not read twice in a day)?
2. Would you allow a one-time copy of the bout histories of the fencers our families compete
   against (roughly the Y12 to Cadet men's foil fields in our region), so the app can seed its
   own opponent notes, after which it would only read on request?
3. Is there an API, a data licence, or a paid tier you would prefer? I would rather pay for a
   proper feed than work around anything.

The app shows no FencingTracker branding, does not republish your pages, and keeps your Strength
number labelled as yours where it is shown. If you would rather we not read the site at all, I
will keep everything manual, as it is today, and say so.

Thank you for building something so many fencing parents rely on.
