Terms of use

What En Garde is

En Garde is a training journal and competition planner for fencing families: a place to log bouts and lessons, keep track of opponents, see where a fencer stands, and plan a season of tournaments and the travel that goes with it. It is run by one fencing family for other fencing families.

It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to USA Fencing, any results service, or any club. Names of organisations and events appear only to describe where a result was earned.

Accounts

  • One parent or guardian holds the family account. That parent may create a login for each of the family's fencers and chooses what a fencer's login can see.
  • The parent is responsible for the family's use of the service, including a fencer's use of a login the parent created.
  • Keep passwords private. Tell us if you think a login has been used by someone else.

Your content

Journal entries, reflections, goals, tournament plans, travel notes and anything else your family types belong to your family. We store them only to run the service for you. You can ask for all of it to be deleted at any time.

Results, rankings and entry lists

Results, rankings, ratings, entry counts and event details are read, on request and one page at a time, from pages that USA Fencing and other results services publish openly, and are kept as a copy so the app stays fast and works away from wifi. They can be late, incomplete or wrong. The official record is always the publisher's own page, and an official standing is whatever USA Fencing says it is.

Costs, flights and hotels

Trip costs, fares and nightly rates are estimates to help a family compare tournaments. They are not offers, and they can change by the hour. Check the real price before booking anything.

Fair use

  • Use En Garde for your own family's fencing.
  • Do not share a login outside the family, copy the service, or use it to collect data about other fencers beyond what the app shows you.
  • Do not use it to harass, rank or publicly compare other children.

No promises

The service is provided as it is, without warranty. It may be changed, paused or withdrawn. Nothing in it is coaching, medical, legal or financial advice. The family that runs it is not liable for decisions made from it, including tournament entries, travel bookings or training choices.

Ending an account

A parent can stop using the service at any time and ask for the family's data to be deleted. We may close an account that breaks these terms.

Privacy

What we hold

  • About the parent: the sign-in email, and the home ZIP code if entered (used only to estimate travel).
  • About each fencer: first name, birth year, weapon, club, and the USA Fencing member number and ranking id that USA Fencing publishes on its public points and ranking pages.
  • Results and standings: placings, points, ratings and entry counts copied from publicly published results pages, keyed to the fencer's public member number.
  • What the family types: bouts, scores, reflections, lessons, goals, opponents notes, tournament plans and travel plans.
  • Technical: sign-in records and the ordinary logs any hosted service keeps.

Children

Fencers using En Garde may be under 13. A fencer never signs up alone: a parent creates the fencer's login, holds the family account, and controls what the fencer's login can see.

We collect nothing from a child beyond what the parent has set up and what the child chooses to write in the journal. There are no advertisements, no tracking, no selling or sharing of a child's information, and no messages sent to a child. A parent can see everything a fencer's login holds and can have all of it deleted.

Where it lives

Data is stored with Supabase in the United States, behind row-level access rules so that each family can read only its own rows. Sign-in is by email and password, or by Google if a family chooses it. There are no third-party analytics or advertising scripts.

Who can see it

  • The family's own logins. The parent sees everything; a fencer sees their own journal and whatever the parent has switched on.
  • The family that runs the service, only as needed to keep it working or when a family asks for help.
  • No one else. We do not sell, rent or share personal information.

Cookies and local storage

The app keeps a sign-in session and a few preferences (the last email used, which fencer is selected, offline changes waiting to sync) in the browser's own storage. Nothing is used to follow you across other sites.

Keeping and deleting

We keep a family's data while the account exists. Ask, and the family's accounts and everything typed into them are deleted. Copies of publicly published results may remain, because they are public records rather than the family's own content.
